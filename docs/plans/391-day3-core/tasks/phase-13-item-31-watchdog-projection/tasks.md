- **Purpose**: two disagreements between what the watchdog SAYS and what it DOES. A seat that never writes a card keeps its birth anchor → the projection is frozen while fires keep landing. - **Goals**: ✅ AC-27 projection == live fire clock, advances per delivered fire · ✅ AC-28 `unknown` → one log line, no watcher notice, no capture; real verdicts unchanged
- **Non-Goals**: ❌ changing fire cadence or `isFireDue` semantics · ❌ touching the status renderer in `cli.ts` · ❌ item 16's `loop.ts` routing (parallel branch) · ❌ descriptor schema
