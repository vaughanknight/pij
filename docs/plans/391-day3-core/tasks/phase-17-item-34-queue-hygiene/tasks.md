(c — o-prime addendum) terminal seats linger as WATCHERS in other seats' `watchdog.json` sidecars (termite still listed for pij-gravitas-shortfall); `pij watchdog status` shows them as live.
- **Goals**: ✅ AC-34 pseudo-seat rows never enqueued (one predicate, registry absence at write); sweep covers every terminal liveness with class-named reasons (`revivePendingAt` exempts only while fresh — newer than the terminal transition AND younger than 1 h); the same sweep drops terminal watcher entries; `pij watchdog status` marks a terminal watcher; `pij queue` stale line (> 24 h) in text + `--json`
