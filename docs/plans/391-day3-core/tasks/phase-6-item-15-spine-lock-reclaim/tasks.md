### Executive Briefing
- **Purpose**: two exclusive-create lock files share one idiom — `adapters/platform-write-lock.ts` (`<pijHome>/spine/write.lock`, budget 5000 ms) and `adapters/spine-store.ts` (`<pijHome>/spine/events.lock`, budget 2000 ms). Both stamp `${pid}:${uuid}` and both refuse forever when the file exists, even if that pid is dead. The daemon's `shutdown` (`daemon.ts:1701-1706`) is `stop?.(); process.exit(0)` — a lock held mid-write at SIGTERM is never released. Fix both layers with ONE shared reclaim rule and release on graceful stop.
- **Goals**: ✅ AC-20 dead-pid reclaim with a spine/receipt note (both layers); live pid still refused; graceful stop leaves no lock · ✅ AC-20b dispatch retire/un-retire append a spine note; `dispatch-retire` says "0 open (N already retired)"
- **Non-Goals**: ❌ changing lock budgets · ❌ stealing a live lock (never) · ❌ the registry RMW race (item 13)
