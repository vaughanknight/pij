## Process lessons (assessment agenda)
- **INS-001** (mandrill, #25 adoption): local-only validation lets defects hide serially behind CI's first failure — #25 was "validated" but never CI-green; four latent layers hid behind the first `npm ci` failure. Encode: require a link to a fully-green CI run before "validated" (adoption/review checklist gate).

- **INS-002** (osk-prime, T3 post-mortem): corroboration ≠ validation when observers share an instrument — 4 seats "confirmed" watchdog pause was a no-op because all 4 read the same lying status display; only an independent live probe (paused seat actually fires/doesn't) was a real test. Encode: when N reports agree, ask whether they share the measuring surface before treating N as evidence.

- **INS-005** (disclosure discipline, mite/mastodon framing): for any artifact containing usage / spend / tokens / credentials — keep the **OBSERVATION** in the tracked/shared artifact (the capability claim, the categories), and the **RAW CAPTURE access-controlled, referenced by path**. Prevents evidence-discipline (keep the raw proof) and disclosure-discipline (don't leak the human's numbers) from pulling against each other — which happened twice this week (the copilot footer carries pij-id + AIU + premium-spend co-located; the observation is useful, the figures are not). o-prime adopts as pij governance default.
- **INS-004** (baton-attribution root cause, takin `faf06c5`): two resolvers answering the same question WILL diverge, and the weaker one leaks into some path — the baton verb used a folder-filtered resolver while the prime primitive used the full-registry one, so the same seat resolved on one path and "operator" on the other. Kin to the event-kind fragmentation. Encode: audit for duplicate answerers of one question; consolidate onto one seam rather than parallel resolvers.
- **INS-003** (hyena/s051 wedge, dove-implicated): a pi seat called `ask_user_question` (modal) — the exact anti-pattern invariant #9 forbids — and wedged for 22h; its turn loop blocked, all sends queued unprocessed. Undetected because its watchdog was `exempt` (collateral of my mass-exemption during the telegram-nudge incident — the deadness detector was off). Two encodes: (a) CLI/harness guard — a pi peer under pij should refuse/redirect `ask_user_question` to inline-send (enforce inv#9 mechanically, not just doctrinally); (b) exemption is not permanent — mass-exemptions need a TTL / re-arm so a safety-off never outlives its incident. Same silent-work-loss family as the codex zombie (T1) but from a modal, not a boot wedge.

- **INS-006** (s3/pregnant-stoat, 2026-07-20; the behavioral twin of T14 Half-2): **a stated intention read back as a completed act, camouflaged by adjacent real completions.** s3 had two errands — the shutdownType/stale-notice findings (sent to mite, delivered) and the axis finding (to dove). Discharging the first *felt like* discharging the obligation; the second stayed something s3 had AGREED to in a message rather than something it had DONE — so it silently never sent, no transport bug. **Second instance of the exact pattern** (identical to reporting a reviewer seat "closed" when the close hadn't run) ⇒ a pattern, not a slip. The camouflage mechanism is the durable part: **both times the false completion sat ADJACENT to real ones**, inside otherwise-accurate traffic, which is exactly what hid it. Two-layer defect with T14: platform side = nothing watches done→released; behavioral side = "I agreed to" gets self-filed as "I did." **Fixes:** (s3) an errand isn't discharged until there's a **receipt** — "I agreed to send it" is not a receipt; (mastodon) an unambiguous instruction ("send it yourself") with an **unverified outcome** is the delegator's half of the same gap — confirm it went. pij already mints send receipts; the discipline is to treat the receipt (not the intention) as the completion. Kin to the whole day's thesis: *done is a claim until verified* — here applied to one's own stated intentions, not just a peer's. Encode: close-out/errand checklists require a receipt or verify-stamp per item; a completion asserted next to real completions gets no adjacency discount.
