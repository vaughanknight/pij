## ⚠ METHOD — READ BEFORE THE FINDINGS

**Cross-seat agreement is counterfeit whenever the agreeing seats share an upstream reader.**
Two probes that converge on a shared source are **one instrument wearing two hats**; diversity
must exist where the reading is TAKEN, not where it is FORMATTED. The test is not *"did we
observe separately?"* but *"where do our paths converge, and what is the first shared thing
upstream of both?"* Everything at or above that join is un-corroborated no matter how many
seats agree — and the more that agree, the more convincing a shared error becomes.

This was established the hard way the same evening
(`doctrine/a-baseline-stores-a-point-not-a-path.md`): two seats, two independently authored
probes, repeated agreement across a boundary, **both wrong**, because both read
`pij anomalies` over one stale field.

**How this survey scores against its own warning — SCORE PER CLAIM-TYPE, NOT PER DOCUMENT.**

corrected it: **subject-diversity is a property of a CLAIM, not of a document**, and this
document contains two kinds with opposite corroboration values. A single score would have been
read across both.)*

| claim type | example | corroboration value |
|---|---|---|
| **"MY PA DID X"** | *"gazelle caught my stale card"*, *"marlin found the unwatched print"* | **GENUINE.** Six primes, six different PAs, six repos, six sets of directly-read output. Paths converge only at the consolidator. Agreement here is real evidence. |

Every seat that agrees inherits the same possible lie. |

**This is the structural finding made flesh**: a prime has no parent, so its errors have no
chaser. The PA is the parent-shaped watcher a prime never had, and that is where all the value
concentrated.

## Does it save tokens/context? Barely — and they said so unprompted

- mastodon: *"tokens displaced from my context ≈ zero; what the PA bought is **latency to
  notice**."*
- roadrunner: *"almost nothing, and I want that unsoftened."*
- meadowlark: *"less than it looks."*
- Only damselfly showed real displaced labour: gate re-verification moved down from prime
  altitude — the exact chore Jordan's ruling was about.

**The honest saves are narrow**: structural inbox enumeration, receipt-classification at
volume, standing polls nobody would otherwise run. **The honest product is latency-to-notice
and error-catching, not token savings.**

## The cost column — three findings, convergent across governments

1. **Frozen-board burn.** gazelle: **~1218 prem for 1 true finding**, ~50 of 72 sweeps
   re-confirming a static board. albatross: ~300 AIU overnight. seahorse: 42/hr until widened
   to 7/hr.
2. **The prime is half the cost.** meadowlark measured **16 of seahorse's 30 wakes were its own
   teaching messages**. roadrunner: *"cheap seat, expensive supervisor — the context burn was
   mine."*
3. **Transcription is where flash tier fails — measured 28/28 vs 0/2.** Chores pointed AT an
   instrument ("run X, paste stdout, classify") ran clean ~28/28. Chores asking the PA to
   re-derive a value produced **composed receipts** — plausible text reconstructed from
   context — in three governments independently. damselfly's law: **point a cheap seat at
   instruments; never make it BE one.**

## Honest negatives, volunteered

- **seahorse reported "0 deltas" truthfully for ~10h over a board with 3 RED PRs.** Reds
  present at briefing became permanent baseline. *Every PA inherits its prime's briefing
  errors, silently.* meadowlark called this the single most valuable thing the survey should
  carry, against its own PA.
- **A PA cannot bound its own disclosure** — the capability gate refuses it the whole watchdog
  family, so its own supervision needs operator hand-edits.
- **3 of 6 PAs keep no status card** while chasing everyone else's.
- **mastodon, against its own charter's headline benefit** — quoted exactly at its request,
  because loose attribution would overstate it: the context reduction was **not yet
  measurable, UNVERIFIED either way**, and it would *"rather report **mechanism adopted,
  benefit not yet measured** than perform a result."* The negative is aimed at the very thing
  the PA programme was sold on.

## Beyond the recipe — every government extended it

damselfly added 5 chores (print-watch corroboration is exportable); mastodon added PR/CI
watching with **named absence traps** ("no CI is not CI-passing"; `UNKNOWN` is not
`CONFLICTING`) that held 72 sweeps with zero drift; roadrunner used its PA as
instrument-of-last-resort on delivery proof. **The misuse cases were all charter-design
faults, self-caught and structurally fixed.**

## What the survey produced downstream, same day

1. **`pij chore`** — the change-detector three governments independently asked for. Specified,
   built (s082), merged (PR #74), rolled out to nine primes and six PAs, and field-tested to
   ~16 defects in one evening.
2. **jaguar's stuck-parked-state workaround unwedged its own o-prime four hours later.** A
   flash-tier PA's field finding, ratified as fleet doctrine in this survey, was the fix for a
   diverged declaration store on the pij o-prime's seat — `report clear` reporting success
   while `node show` still read `waiting`. **The cheap seat's finding repaired the expensive
   seat that commissioned the survey.**

## Recommendations

1. **Change-detector before full sweep** — no-delta collapses to a one-line tick in a log
   file; message the prime only on delta. *(Shipped as `pij chore`.)*
2. **Periodic full-state report** — "8 PRs: 5 green, 3 RED", fixing delta-blindness.
3. **Charter rule: classification-and-relay over pasted receipts, never re-derivation.**
4. **Grant role `pa` the watchdog verb family** — a supervision instrument that cannot manage
   its own subscriptions makes every fix above it manual. *(Jordan's ruling.)*
5. **PAs must keep their own cards.**
